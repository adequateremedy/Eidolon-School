How to Play

Drag each item from the belt into a packing box.

A box only takes the items pictured on its tally board, in the amounts shown. Fill every line on a box's board and the box ships — 50 points each.

Items wearing a red no-pack badge are junk in Rounds 3 and 4. Never pack those — let them ride on by. In Round 5 they become real items and the badges come off.

If an item a box still needs reaches the end of the belt, it circles back to the start, so nothing is ever lost.

Ship every box in all 5 rounds to finish the class with 2,000 points.
